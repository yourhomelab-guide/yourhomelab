Self-hosting introduces a lot of new terms. Some are abbreviations; others sound familiar but have specific meanings in this context. This glossary explains them briefly and without requiring any prior knowledge.

In the articles and guides on this site, the first occurrence of a term is underlined with a dotted line. Clicking on it takes you directly to the corresponding entry here. For more details, many terms have their own article in the ["Reference"](/en/wiki/) section.
