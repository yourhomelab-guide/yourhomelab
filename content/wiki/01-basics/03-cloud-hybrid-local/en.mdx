## The three models

<Cards>
  <Card title="Local">
    The server is at your home, for example a mini PC or an old laptop. Your data never leaves the house.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid">
    The data stays at home, and a small cloud server or a tunnel takes the requests from the internet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cloud">
    You rent a virtual server (VPS) in a data center. Fixed IP, no device at home, monthly costs.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Compared

|                  | Local                            | Hybrid                                  | Cloud                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Costs**        | Hardware once, then power        | Hardware plus a small VPS               | Monthly rent                   |
| **Data ownership** | Entirely with you              | Data at home, entry point at a provider | With the provider              |
| **Reachability** | Depends on your internet line    | Good, even without a public IPv4        | Very good, fixed IP            |
| **Effort**       | Medium                           | Higher, two systems                     | Low                            |
| **Good for**     | Photos, media, large amounts of data | Public services with data at home   | Small services, first steps    |
