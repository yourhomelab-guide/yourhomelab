## The three models

<Cards>
  <Card title="Local">
    The server sits in your home, for example a mini PC or an old laptop. Your data never leaves the house.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid">
    The data lives at home, while a small cloud server or a tunnel accepts requests from the internet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cloud">
    You rent a virtual private server (VPS) in a data center. Fixed IP, no device at home, monthly costs.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Compared

|                    | Local                              | Hybrid                                | Cloud                           |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Cost**           | Hardware once, then electricity    | Hardware plus a small VPS             | Monthly rent                    |
| **Data ownership** | Entirely yours                     | Data at home, entry point at provider | With the provider               |
| **Reachability**   | Depends on your internet connection | Good, even without public IPv4       | Very good, fixed IP             |
| **Effort**         | Medium                             | Higher, two systems                   | Low                             |
| **Good for**       | Photos, media, large amounts of data | Public services with data at home   | Small services, getting started |
