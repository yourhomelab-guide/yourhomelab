## The Three Models

<Cards>
  <Card title="Local">
    The server is located in your home—perhaps a mini-PC or an old laptop. Your data never leaves your home.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid">
    The data is stored at home; a small cloud server or a tunnel handles requests from the Internet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cloud">
    You rent a virtual server (VPS) in a data center. Static IP, no hardware at home, monthly fees.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## In comparison

|                 | Local                            | Hybrid                                 | Cloud                          |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Costs**      | Hardware: one-time cost; after that, electricity  | Hardware plus a small VPS              | Monthly Rent               |
| **Data Sovereignty** | Completely with you              | Data at home, received by the provider   | At the provider's                  |
| **Accessibility** | It depends on the connection        | Well, even without a public IPv4 address        | Very good, static IP             |
| **Effort**     | Medium                           | Higher, two systems                    | Low                         |
| **Good for**     | Photos, media, large amounts of data | Public Services Using Data at Home  | Small Services, Getting Started |
