---
question: Where else can I find help and services?
---
[awesome-selfhosted](https://awesome-selfhosted.net) is a big list of free software you can host yourself. [selfh.st](https://selfh.st) offers news and directories, [r/selfhosted](https://www.reddit.com/r/selfhosted/) and [r/homelab](https://www.reddit.com/r/homelab/) are active communities. For many containers, [LinuxServer.io](https://www.linuxserver.io) is worth a look too.
